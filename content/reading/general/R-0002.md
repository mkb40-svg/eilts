# Training and Development: A Guide for Staff

**A** **Induction for new employees.** All new staff members take part in an induction programme during their first two weeks with the company. The programme begins with a half-day welcome session led by the Human Resources team, which covers pay arrangements, working hours and the main company policies. After this, each new employee is assigned a workplace buddy, an experienced colleague from the same department who can answer everyday questions and help them settle in. Your buddy will also show you around the site during your first week. New staff must also complete the online health and safety course before they are allowed to use any equipment in the warehouse. At the end of the first month, line managers will arrange a short review meeting to discuss how the employee is getting on and whether any further training is needed.

**B** **Compulsory training.** Some courses are required for all employees, regardless of their role. These include fire safety, data protection and manual handling, which teaches staff how to lift and carry heavy items without injury. Most of these courses are delivered in the training room on the ground floor, although some can be completed online. Compulsory courses must be renewed every two years, and staff will receive an email reminder six weeks before their certificate expires. Employees who work with forklift trucks or other specialist machinery must hold a valid operator's licence, and the company will pay the full cost of obtaining and renewing it. Compulsory training always takes place during normal working hours, so no one will be asked to attend in their own time.

**C** **Optional courses and qualifications.** In addition to compulsory training, staff are encouraged to develop new skills that will help them progress within the company. A list of approved courses, ranging from basic spreadsheet skills to team leadership, is available on the staff intranet. To apply, employees should fill in an application form and discuss it with their line manager, who must approve the request before a place can be booked. For longer courses leading to a recognised qualification, the company may contribute up to half of the fees. Staff who receive this support and then leave the company within twelve months of completing the course may be asked to repay part of the amount.

**D** **Study leave.** Employees studying for an approved qualification are entitled to up to five days of paid study leave per year. This leave may be used to attend classes, prepare for examinations or sit the examinations themselves. Study leave is separate from your normal holiday allowance. Requests for study leave should be made at least four weeks in advance so that managers can arrange cover. Please note that study leave cannot be carried over into the following year, and any days not used by the end of December will be lost.

**E** **Recording and reviewing your training.** All training, whether compulsory or optional, is recorded in your personal training log, which you can view at any time on the intranet. It is your responsibility to upload copies of any certificates you receive from external providers. Your training log will be discussed at your annual performance review, where you and your manager will agree on your development goals for the coming year. Managers may also use training logs to identify gaps in their team's skills and to plan future courses. If you have any questions about training, please contact the Learning and Development Coordinator, whose office is on the first floor of the main building.
